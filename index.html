01 / The introduction

A little about me.

Hello! I'm Abeer, an Engineering Physcis student at McMaster University, interested in understanding how things work and finding ways to make them better.

I'm particularly interested in mechanical systems and photonics. I enjoy exploring new ideas, working through challenging problems, and turning concepts into something tangible.

Outside of academics, I enjoy reading fictional as well as non-fictional books, learning about outer space, and puzzles. This little corner of the internet is where I keep track of the things I've worked on and the things I've learned.
